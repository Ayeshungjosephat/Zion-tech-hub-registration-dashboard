# Zion Tech Hub Registration & Community Growth Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes registration data from Zion Tech Hub to understand
community growth, cohort performance, referral channels, program demand,
and registration trends.

The dashboard was developed in Power BI to provide a clear view of where
registrations are coming from and which programs and audiences show the
strongest levels of interest.

## Business Problem

Zion Tech Hub is preparing for a paid advertising campaign and needs to
understand its existing registration patterns before allocating its first
advertising budget.

The analysis focuses on two key questions:

1. Where should the advertising budget be allocated, and which audiences
   should be targeted?
2. What can be done differently to increase community growth and Cohort 11
   registrations?

## Key Metrics

- Total Registrations: 1,318
- Countries: 46
- Programs: 7
- Referral Channels: 7
- Cohort 9 Registrations: 1,147
- Cohort 10 Registrations: 171

## Key Findings

### Referral Channels

X (Twitter) generated the highest number of registrations, followed by
LinkedIn and WhatsApp.

This suggests that referral-channel performance should be considered when
planning the first paid advertising campaign.

### Program Demand

The highest registration volumes came from:

- Data Science and AI
- Healthcare Data Analytics
- Financial Analytics
- Sales and Marketing Analytics

These programs represent important audience segments for future campaigns.

### Cohort Performance

Cohort 9 recorded significantly more registrations than Cohort 10.

The registration covered 4 months, Jan & Feb and Nov & Dec while cohort 10 covered just 2 months, May & June. 
Jan & Nov had the most registration, this suggest more person possibly consider changing career path or take up new challenge of learning a new skill.

## Assumptions made 

Occupation was excluded from the analysis because occupation information was only available for a small sample of Cohort 10 registrations,
while the majority of registrations came from Cohort 9, where occupation data was largely unavailable.
Including occupation would therefore provide a limited and potentially unrepresentative view of the overall registration population. This was seen as a data limitation 

## Recommendations
1. Budget allocation
Channel	Budget	Audience focus
X (Twitter)	65%	Data Science & AI, Healthcare
LinkedIn	15%	Data Science & AI, Financial Analytics
WhatsApp	10%	Healthcare, Data Science & AI
Referral	5%	Existing registrants/alumni
Facebook	3%	Broad program awareness
Instagram	2%	Awareness/younger audiences

2. Start campaign before peak registration months (January & November) and Mid-week in this months
3. A reward program for referrals can help boost community
4. Track advertising spend and cost per registration once paid campaigns begins
